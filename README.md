# VoltPayback PRO — EV Charging & Home Solar Payback Calculator

A fast, interactive, zero-login financial and energy calculator that estimates Level 1 vs. Level 2 EV charging efficiency loss, Time-of-Use (TOU) rate savings, annual gas vehicle savings, and home solar payback period (factoring in the 30% Federal Investment Tax Credit).

## ⚡ Features & Physics Engine
- **Level 1 vs Level 2 Efficiency Loss:** Factors in 15% thermal energy loss on 120V Level 1 wall outlets vs 10% loss on 240V Level 2 chargers.
- **Time-of-Use (TOU) Rate Scheduler:** Real-time calculation of overnight off-peak power rates vs standard on-peak rates.
- **30% Federal ITC Tax Credit:** Automatic tax credit calculation & net payback timeline in years.
- **Interactive 10-Year SVG Cash Flow Chart:** Real-time visual comparison of cumulative costs between Gas, Grid EV, and Solar EV.
- **Vehicle Presets:** Built-in specs for Tesla Model Y, Model 3, Ford F-150 Lightning, Chevy Bolt, Hyundai Ioniq 5, and Custom EV.
- **100% Free & Private:** Zero email address or login required.

## 🛠️ Technology Stack
- **HTML5 & CSS3:** Responsive glassmorphic dark design system with custom range sliders.
- **Vanilla JavaScript:** Zero external JS library dependencies for maximum performance and fast load speed.
- **Micro-Interactions:** Scroll-triggered picture frame transform animations and 3D hover effects.
